<h1> Using a classifier with ampliConda </h1>
There are plenty of pre-trained classifiers for a number of different uses depending on your own project goals.
The QIIME2 website (https://library.qiime2.org/data-resources) can be a good place to start.  


**Note that you must use a classifier compatible with the proper version of QIIME2 in this workflow (2026.1)**
**You may also train your own classifier if you wish, but ensure that you train it on the 2026.1 version of QIIME2 to ensure compatibility!**
